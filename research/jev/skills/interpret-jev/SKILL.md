# Interpret Jev evidence

Jev supplies a provisional interpretation of market conditions. Hermes owns the
trade thesis and decision. Read the Jev briefing first when it is available, then
challenge it against the supplied price path, location, actual liquidity, structure
and position facts. Concentrate on whether a tradeable path exists from this entry
location, what invalidates it, what movement is allowed, and what costs consume.
Rebuilding every oscillator consensus is unnecessary when it adds no relevant
agreement, contradiction or missing evidence to that decision.

Match each answer to its event, anchor and horizon. UP/DOWN/FLAT concerns an endpoint
relative to the explicit neutral band; it is not original-target-before-stop
probability. Continuation/reversal is relative to the stated past movement, not
the desired position. A strong current trend and a neutral future endpoint can
coexist. A reversal estimate is not an instruction to reverse a trade.

Use the full distributions. Concentration measures the model's certainty, not
observed trading accuracy. These answers share evidence and are correlated; never
multiply them as independent votes, average unrelated horizons, or turn a fixed
probability threshold into trade permission. Agreement with a duplicated indicator
is not a second independent confirmation. Experimental probabilities have no
automatic priority over contradictory native evidence.

For a position, assess its original authored thesis, permitted pullback and current
native protection. Endpoint color, one adverse tick or P&L giveback alone cannot
establish thesis failure. Keep sampled excursion distinct from executable proceeds.
Hermes still evaluates continuation versus exit with actual geometry and costs.

When advice is stale, missing, inapplicable or uncertain, continue from the supplied
market facts and state the uncertainty. Never invent omitted indicators or wait
for a fresh inference. In the existing audit, briefly identify which Jev judgment
was useful, challenged or irrelevant and why. Preserve the current output schema
and all native action/geometry contracts. This skill authorizes no tool or order.
