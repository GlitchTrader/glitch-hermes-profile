# Market Structure Playbook

Use this skill during review and learning to name evidence. It is not loaded
on the direct trading hot path and it never computes an action, quantity, stop,
target, or veto.

- Range: compare edge rejection and sweep behavior with the opposite edge.
- Directional: prefer pullbacks into accepted structure with room to the next
  liquidity objective.
- Transition: reduce exposure or remain flat when location and invalidation are
  unclear, while allowing a bounded anticipatory thesis before full confirmation.
- Distinguish acceptance from a sweep-and-reclaim. A first poke is not proof of
  continuation, but it can be evidence when location, room, and invalidation
  are coherent.
- Compare stop distance with ordinary MNQ noise and the actual objective. A
  cosmetic 1:1 scalp inside noise is not an edge.
- A recent loss near the same zone is not a direction signal. Re-entry needs
  materially new evidence: reclaim, deeper sweep, or regime change.
