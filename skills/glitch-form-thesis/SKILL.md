# Form Thesis

Judge bull, bear, and flat cases from current observable evidence. Aggressive and conservative cases are perspectives, not separate strategies.

1. When flat, evaluate long, short, and flat symmetrically for the most likely next-five-minute path - not the next fifteen minutes - while treating the immediate and next one-minute movement as the primary entry-timing object. Flat is the current state, not the preferred choice. When positioned and reviewing each minute, forecast the most likely next one-minute candle and manage from that forecast, structure, and risk. Use 15m/60m as context and opposing-risk awareness, never as permission or short-horizon direction; use 1m/5m as timing and noise.
2. Consider multiple setup families—continuation, pullback, breakout, mean reversion, scalp, or transition—without requiring or forcing any archetype.
3. Describe whether the move is initiating, progressing, or exhausting, but treat those labels, session extremes, support, resistance, and "late" as evidence rather than vetoes. A retest is optional, not required confirmation. Compare remaining plausible movement from the current price with local structural risk and act when either direction retains bounded positive expectancy. `NOTHING` carries the same burden of proof: state why neither direction qualifies now.
4. Define invalidation before target and match it to the one-to-five-minute thesis. Anchor the stop beyond the nearest local structure that genuinely invalidates the expected movement plus observable one-minute noise, not a distant higher-timeframe pivot or a convenient offset. Never use `compact`, `tight`, `defined risk`, or an attractive R multiple to place it inside ordinary one-minute movement.
5. Choose a target that the stated horizon, structure, and regime can plausibly reach. Do not select a distant target merely to make reward/risk look good. If valid risk is too large, use a smaller supplied quantity or remain flat.
6. After a stop or failed managed exit, require materially changed price, structure, or immediate behavior before nearby same-direction re-entry and state exactly what changed. A small reclaim or repeated narrative near the same level is churn.
7. For open positions, judge favorable excursion, rollback, and remaining opportunity in initial-risk units and structure. Native stops are catastrophe protection, not passive management.
8. `HOLD` and `NOTHING` carry the same burden of proof as every other action. When current evidence satisfies the prior review's `change_condition`, choose the newly supported action or identify genuinely new contrary evidence; price following the forecast is not sufficient reason to move the threshold.

Choose `ENTER_LONG`, `ENTER_SHORT`, `HOLD`, `MOVE_STOP`, `MOVE_TP`, `EXIT`, or `NOTHING`. Exact-leg `MOVE_TP` may extend or reduce selected remaining opportunities and may carry an exact-leg stop amendment. Pass only a compact factual `decision_audit`; never reveal private chain-of-thought.
