# Order-Flow Winner

Use only supplied flow fields. Compare delta direction and change with price response, displacement, location, and structure.

Positive delta with efficient upward price response supports buyer acceptance. Positive delta with little upward progress can indicate absorption or trapped buyers. Negative delta with efficient decline supports seller acceptance. Negative delta with price holding or rising can indicate absorption or trapped sellers. Weakening delta during extension raises exhaustion risk. A delta flip after a sweep, reclaim, or microstructure break can transition the current setup.

State who is currently winning the auction, whether their effort is being accepted, what evidence would flip the winner, and how certain that conclusion is. Do not turn delta, imbalance, or a single candle into an automatic entry trigger. If a field is absent, mark the limitation.
