# Learn From Glitch

Current native Glitch facts outrank derived state, guidance, memory, labels, and inference. Learn only from completed master outcomes. Correlated Sim101/Sim301 or route copies are one market idea unless independence is established; follower results diagnose replication, not strategy quality.

The persisted `INSTRUMENT_COMPARISON_V1` ledger is part of the ex-ante decision evidence. For every episode, preserve and score the MNQ/MES/M2K candidate blocks separately:

- bullish setup/path and status;
- bearish setup/path and status;
- current setup and phase;
- next setup and transition trigger;
- objective, invalidation, room, and asymmetry;
- order-flow winner and price-response agreement;
- candidate rank and selected instrument;
- actual subsequent path and outcome.

A `NOTHING` decision is not a missing episode. It is a global comparison whose rejected candidates can later be evaluated against each candidate's own subsequent five-bar path. Do not score only the selected/reference instrument. When the ex-ante packet and ledger contain enough structure, reconstruct one conservative noise-aware counterfactual zone, genuine invalidation, and probabilistic objective for each plausible rejected direction, then evaluate target-before-stop chronology on the later authoritative path. This is opportunity classification, not a fabricated fill or PnL. Lack of an actual trade outcome or model-emitted bracket does not exempt repeated `NOTHING` decisions from missed-opportunity review. Distinguish supported probabilistic room from arbitrary extension; do not require a future target to have traded before the hypothetical entry.

Use AI-origin completed master outcomes for self-evaluation. Group correlated master records from the same cycle, instrument, and action as one market idea. Manual trades remain external context unless explicitly tagged for imitation. Separate market interpretation, instrument ranking, entry geometry, position management, execution/replication, data availability, policy rejection, and infrastructure. Never turn an infrastructure failure into a trading lesson.

Evaluate ex ante forecasts when present: continuation, reversal, target-before-stop, next 5–10-candle path, regime, and setup transition. Track calibration and sample eligibility separately from realized PnL. Compare initial risk in points, ticks, one- and five-minute ATR or equivalent supplied horizon noise, one-contract dollars, friction, and actual time to stop. Test whether apparent asymmetry came from a stop denominator too small to survive the forecast horizon, and keep that diagnosis separate from direction selection and position management. Compare the realized path against both the selected candidate and rejected alternatives whenever the packet supports bounded counterfactual geometry. Never fabricate fills or PnL, but do classify target-before-stop chronology and repeated effective opportunity cost.

Promote only repeated comparable evidence with contrary-evidence review, one conditional adjustment, an evaluation metric, and rollback condition. Guidance must be compact, falsifiable, reversible, and subordinate to current evidence. Never promote fixed quantity, stop distance, ATR multiple, automatic breakeven, forced session recipe, daily quota, martingale, grid, or blind averaging-down rules.

A cognitive overlay may change only the designated learned clause and must preserve authority, scope, action, identity, JSON, protection, execution, and risk text. Logging, debriefing, generated plans, and status changes are not proof of cognition improvement.
