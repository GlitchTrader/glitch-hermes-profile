# v0.0.2.76 — decision-output reliability

This release repairs output preparation and serialization without changing
trading strategy, probability estimates, geometry, execution ranges, native
protection, replication, risk limits, model selection or worker cadence.

## Warranted changes

1. For an already-authored NOTHING, calculate a contradictory bare terminal EV
   label from unchanged levels, costs and probability range. Unknown or qualified
   evidence stays untouched. Entry and management contradictions retain their
   existing strict checks. Positive arithmetic does not force an entry.
2. Relocate a complete terminal audit tail when its repeated final choice is
   identical to the canonical field. Conflicts and partial tails remain invalid.
3. Give format correction the authoritative ledger mode and ordered-master count,
   avoiding an instrument-per-decision or wrong-mode correction.
4. Correct the conflicting instruction that allowed only fields from a base
   HOLD/NOTHING template despite action contracts requiring extra entry/protection
   fields. The template governs shared shape, not omission of required fields.
5. Reject missing or invalid protection payloads without an unusable second call:
   the existing management boundary prohibits adding or changing a native mutation
   during evidence-free repair. A fresh full-state review remains necessary.
   Do not infer a stop from prose or invent a leg identity.
