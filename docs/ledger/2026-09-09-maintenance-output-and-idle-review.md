# v0.0.2.77 — management serialization and native-locked idle scans

Two mechanical changes are authorized by the daily maintenance review. No
cognition text, probability estimate, chosen action, geometry, native execution,
protection, replication, risk setting, model, learner or scheduler is changed.

## Causal scope

1. A management arithmetic correction produced a correct single verdict followed
   by a parenthetical calculation. The bare-enum validator rejected the response.
   Accept one unambiguous verdict with an optional nonempty parenthetical while
   preserving the audit verbatim and retaining the probability/break-even checks.
   Contradictory verdicts, placeholders and changed management payloads remain
   subject to the existing strict checks and bounded correction rules.
2. Flat scans continued after the selected master had reached its existing
   configured native daily-capture entry lock. Read the native portfolio snapshot
   with the same ten-second freshness bound used by native capture admission.
   Skip only new decision work when every selected master explicitly has capture
   enabled, available and reached, and every enabled member of those books has
   available native state, no position and zero working orders. Missing, stale or
   ambiguous facts retain the normal path. One eligible master, any open exposure,
   any working order or an explicit operator instruction also retains that path.
   Recheck before a model call after lock waiting. Pending delivery runs first.

The idle optimization does not create a profit target, trading gate, pause latch,
or synthetic decision. It follows the user's already-enforced native setting and
automatically ceases when fresh native facts change. Learner admission is unchanged.

## Proof and release boundary

Focused tests cover the observed management annotation, all four management
actions, strict arithmetic, malformed labels, existing correction limits, native
capture scope/freshness/unknown-state handling, next-session eligibility and
existing delivery precedence and native capture changing while awaiting a model
lock. Validation passed: 485 profile tests, 37 adjacent native/control tests,
53 compiled native safety checks and 176 host-recovery checks. Offline replay of
the observed corrected management response fails under v76 and passes under v77
without changing the authored batch; no provider call was made.

Publication, manifest parity, preserved configuration/learning, and a natural
new-version cycle are separate checks. The private operational review and native
financial reconciliation remain outside this public repository. A passing
regression suite or one natural cycle is not proof of profitability or zero defects.

Rollback is the checkpointed prior distribution through the supported updater,
with AI paused while installed files change and only its prior state restored.
Do not roll back native code, accounts, orders, positions, learning or epoch data.
